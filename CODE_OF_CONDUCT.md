# Community conduct

ADS welcomes contributors regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Be respectful, make room for different perspectives, and discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks. Give constructive feedback and accept corrections.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, impersonation, disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces managed by the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban repeat or severe offenders. Decisions should reflect impact and context, and protect the privacy of affected people. A maintainer involved in an incident should not decide its outcome alone where another uninvolved maintainer is available.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content. For a project-specific concern, request a private contact from a maintainer without publishing sensitive details; this project does not yet designate a separate private conduct inbox. Share incident details only after a private channel has been established. Retaliation against a reporter or anyone participating in a review is unacceptable.
